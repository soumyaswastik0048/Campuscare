Streamlined Lifecycle

How CampusCare Works

From anonymous submission to verified closure, transparent accountability at every stage.

1. Verified Anonymous Submission

Students verify using their college credentials. The complaint is immediately decoupled from the student’s identity and stored with a unique anonymous UUID tracking ID.

2. Admin Action & Escalation

Hostel wardens and mess supervisors manage issues through their dashboard. Overdue complaints trigger automatic reminders and escalate up the administration chain.

3. Student Resolution Confirmation

Admins cannot unilaterally mark an issue closed without verification. The student receives the resolution update and confirms if the problem was genuinely solved.

Key Features

Engineered for Campus Accountability

Designed specifically to bridge communication gaps between students and campus administration.

Category-Aware Evidence

Physical issues like broken taps or power sockets recommend photo proof, while experiential concerns like food taste or staff conduct do not block submission.

Similar Issue Grouping

Intelligently clusters recurring complaints across blocks, helping administration pinpoint campus-wide outages or repeat catering problems.

Real-Time Analytics

Live breakdown of pending, active, and resolved cases with metrics on resolution efficiency, category distributions, and overdue tracking.

Privacy Architecture

Identity Verification vs. Complaint Anonymity

We strictly uphold student trust by decoupling authentication from grievance records.

1. Identity Verification

Ensures only genuine college students access the platform. Prevents external spam or malicious submissions while maintaining an abuse-free campus community.

2. Zero Identity Leakage

Administrators see zero personal student data. No names, no roll numbers, and no student emails appear in grievance records or dashboard lists.
